[5.1] However, regarding the examples of ancient times, let us stop here; let us look at the athletes nearest to us, choosing noble examples from our generation.
[5.2] Because of jealousy and envy, those greatest and most righteous pillars suffered persecution, struggling until death.
[5.3] Let us set before our eyes those good apostles:
[5.4] Peter, because of unrighteous jealousy, suffered not once, not twice, but many times; having thus borne witness, he went to the place of glory he deserved.
[5.5] Because of jealousy and strife, Paul showed the reward of patience:
[5.6] He was seven times in chains, was stoned, was driven out, became a herald in the east and the west, and received the noble reputation of his faith;
[5.7] He taught righteousness to the whole world, and came to the end of the west, bearing witness before the rulers; thus he departed from the world and was received into the holy place, becoming the greatest example of patience.
