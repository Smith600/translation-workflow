[53.1] For you have understanding, you have a good understanding of the sacred Scriptures, beloved, and you have studied the oracles of God. Therefore we write these things to remind you.
[53.2] For when Moses went up into the mountain, and passed forty days and forty nights in fasting and humiliation, God said to him:—‘*Go down hence quickly, for thy seople, whom thou didst bring out of the land of gypt, have committed iniquity ; they have quickly rone aside out of the way which thou _ didst sommand them; they have made themselves molten mages.”
[53.3] And the Lord said to him :—“ I have poken to thee once and twice, saying, I have seen his people, and behold it is stiffmecked; suffer me to destroy them, and 1 will wipe out their name from under heaven, and thee will I make into a nation great and wonderful and much more than this.”
[53.4] And Moses said, “ Not so, Lord; pardon the sin of this people, or blot me also out of the book of the living.”
[53.5] O great love! O unsurpassable perfection! The servant is bold with the Lord, he asks forgiveness for the people, or begs that he himself may be blotted out together with them.
