[14.1] Tuererore it is right and holy, my brethren, for us to obey God rather than to follow those who in pride and unruliness are the instigators of an abominable jealousy.
[14.2] For we shall incur no common harm, but great danger, if we rashly yield ourselves to the purposes of men who rush _ into strife and sedition, to estrange us from what is right.
[14.3] Let us be kind to one another, according to the compassion and sweetness of our maker.
[14.4] For it is written, “The kind shall inhabit the land, and the guiltless shall be left on it, but they who transgress shall be destroyed from off it.”
[14.5] And again he says: “I saw the ungodly lifted high, and exalted as the cedars of Lebanon. And I Is. 29. ve Mk. 7, 6 went by, and behold he was not; and I sought his place, and I found it not. Keep innocence, and look on uprightness; for there is a remnant for a peaceable man.”
